The Problem

Where your margin
goes to die

Mid-size GCs run on email, Excel, and phone calls. The $10T construction industry still has almost zero software intelligence where it matters — in the field, on the drawings, on the deadline.

01
Change Order Chaos
COs live in email threads. Scope creep is invisible until it's a legal dispute. No pricing intelligence, no audit trail, no accountability chain. 3–10% of every contract value bleeds out untracked.
⚠ 3–10% contract value disputed per project
02
RFI Black Holes
Each RFI that triggers rework spawns 2–4 follow-up RFIs. Teams work on incorrect assumptions because nobody tracks who has the ball. Field crews build the wrong thing — twice.
⚠ $1,080 processing cost per RFI
03
Manual Bid Estimation
A 20,000 sq ft TI project needs 12–20 hours of senior estimator time. At $95/hr, that's $1,900 in labor per bid — before a single sub has quoted. Miscounts compound into margin-killing losses before the first shovel hits.
⚠ $90K+/yr estimating labor at 40 bids/yr
04
Compliance Blind Spots
OSHA now mandates digital injury logs on 20+ worker sites. Permit requirements vary by municipality. Safety docs live in spreadsheets, email, and filing cabinets — not auditable, not searchable, not defensible.
⚠ 8–15% GL insurance premium overpayment
